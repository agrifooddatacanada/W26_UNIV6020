**Name**: UNIV6020 Sample  
**Description**: This schema describes bycatch counts of four species of natural enemies of the Colorado potato beetle. These counts came from Uni-Co traps that were placed in a commercial potato field located in Elora, Ontario. The traps were baited with lures that had Colorado potato beetle aggregate pheromone release rates of 0.5mg/day, 1mg/day, 2mg/day, and a control.
