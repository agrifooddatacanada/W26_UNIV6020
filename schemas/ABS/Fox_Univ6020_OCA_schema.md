**Name**: Univ6020 Assn  
**Description**: This project evaluates the effects of dietary yeast autolysate supplementation on gut permeability, heat stress, and performance in high-performance sled dogs. Using controlled feeding and exercise trials, the study assesses physiological, metabolic, and gastrointestinal responses under physical stress. The outcomes will identify whether yeast supplementation improves intestinal barrier integrity, thermoregulation, and overall performance. Findings are intended to inform evidence-based dietary strategies for working and athletic dogs.
